# Community conduct

MARS-3 welcomes respectful, technically rigorous participation. Contributors
must communicate without harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, doxxing, or deliberate disruption. Challenge ideas and evidence,
not people. Respect privacy, source licenses, coordinated vulnerability
disclosure, and the public-repository boundary.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate these
expectations and may restrict participation for repeated or serious behavior.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately when they
contain sensitive safety information, and avoid unnecessary disclosure of a
reporter or affected person.

Report security issues through `SECURITY.md`. Report conduct concerns to the
repository owner through a private GitHub channel. Do not place personal or
sensitive evidence in a public issue.
